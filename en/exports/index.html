Exports

We review buyer enquiries from international markets, including China, based on specifications, quantities, packing and documentation requirements.

Export products

Sesame seeds

Sudanese sesame is available in white or red varieties and packed in sacks to buyer requirements. Grade, purity, moisture and impurity levels are agreed with the buyer. Shipments must be free of insects, mould and abnormal odours.

Groundnuts

Sudanese groundnuts are available in-shell or shelled, with packaging to buyer requirements. Kernels must be sound, clean, uniform and free of mould. Moisture and impurity levels are agreed with the buyer, subject to applicable food safety and aflatoxin limits in the destination market.
